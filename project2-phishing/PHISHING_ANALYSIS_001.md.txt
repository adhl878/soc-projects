2. SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication failures
3. Urgency language and artificial deadlines
4. Requests for sensitive information (password reset required)
5. Suspicious sender IP not in Amazon's range
6. Generic greeting ("Dear Customer")
7. Executable file attachment (.exe)
8. Grammar issues ("Verify your identify")
9. Links shortened/obfuscated with URL parameters
10. Threatening language about account closure
